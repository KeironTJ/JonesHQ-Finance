Cookies and browser storage

Family HQ uses cookies to support sign-in and protect forms, and browser storage to remember display preferences. No advertising or analytics trackers are included in the application.

Sign-in and security cookies

CookiePurposeLifetime
session Signed session state, including sign-in, form protection, security challenges, temporary reset-link tokens and on-screen messages. Signed does not mean encrypted. A browser-session cookie by default. Server-side login limits also apply independently.
remember_token Keeps you signed in when you choose Remember Me. Up to {{ config.REMEMBER_COOKIE_DURATION.days }} days by default, unless the session expires or is revoked earlier.

In production, these cookies are Secure (HTTPS only), HttpOnly (not accessible to page scripts) and SameSite=Lax. Ordinary sign-ins have an idle limit of {{ config.SESSION_IDLE_MINUTES }} minutes and an absolute limit of {{ config.SESSION_ABSOLUTE_HOURS }} hours. Remember Me sessions have a separate maximum lifetime. You can revoke signed-in devices from Account security.

Appearance saved to your account

When signed in, your colour palette and light, dark or automatic display mode are saved to your user account and follow you across devices. They are personal, not household settings. Browser theme storage does not override your account.

Display preferences stored in your browser

  • jhq-theme: the light, dark or automatic display mode for signed-out pages only. The signed-out palette is always Blue.
  • categories.openSections and vendors.openSections: identifiers for expanded sections on those pages.

These local-storage preferences have no automatic expiry and remain after signing out. They are browser-specific, not account-specific. Clear this site's data in your browser settings to remove them. Clearing cookies will also sign this browser out. Blocking cookies prevents normal sign-in and form protection; your browser may restore session cookies when restoring tabs.

External services and account emails

The application serves its styles, scripts, icons and chart library locally and uses your device's system fonts. It does not need Google Fonts or a public asset CDN for these resources.

When outgoing email is configured, the configured email provider processes recipient addresses and account messages such as confirmation links, password resets and security alerts. Security alerts may include the time, IP address and browser description. This is server-side email delivery, not a browser tracking cookie.

This notice describes the application's cookies and browser storage. It is not a complete privacy policy for financial records, operational logs, backups, or services added by the site operator. See the privacy notice for wider data handling and deletion limitations.
