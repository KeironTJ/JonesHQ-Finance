Permanently delete your account

This cannot be undone in the app. Your sign-in account, sessions, recovery codes and privately owned records will be removed. This does not delete the household.
  • Private accounts/cards and their transactions, private loans/payments, pensions/snapshots, vehicles/fuel/trips, income, expenses and plans/items will be deleted. Owned records in a former household are included.
  • Shared financial records remain, including bank entries linked to private income, expenses or loan/card payments. Links to deleted records are removed, but amounts, balances, names and free text in shared history are not anonymised.
  • Invites you created to join a family are deleted; pending household-registration invites you issued are revoked. Your prior security events remain under the normal retention policy, without their user-account link. They may still contain identifying details such as IP addresses or free text.
  • Backups, operational logs, email-provider records and copies previously downloaded by others are not erased by this action.
  • The last active household administrator must appoint another active administrator first. Site admins must revoke their site-admin role on the server first.

Export your data from Account security before continuing. Browser display preferences remain until you clear this site's storage in your browser.
