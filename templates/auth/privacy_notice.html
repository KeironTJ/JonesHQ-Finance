Privacy notice

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Who operates this service

Family HQ is operated by Keiron Jones, the controller responsible for personal data processed to run this service. The application server is hosted in the United Kingdom. The service is intended for public use, with new-household registration currently controlled by invitations.

For privacy questions, corrections or requests, email keirontj@gmail.com. Do not email passwords, authenticator secrets, recovery codes or full financial exports. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

What information is processed and where it comes from

  • Accounts and households: names, email addresses, household membership, display names, roles, section permissions and invitations.
  • Financial and household records: information you or other household members enter or import, such as accounts, transactions, balances, income, expenses, loans, credit cards, pensions, property, mortgages, vehicles, mileage, budgets and plans. Childcare features can include children's names, school year groups, activities, dates and costs. Descriptions and free text may identify other people.
  • Authentication and security: password hashes, two-factor configuration, hashed recovery codes and session tokens, sign-in and security events, IP addresses, browser descriptions and timestamps.
  • Technical and support information: requests handled by the server/proxy, operational errors, rate-limit information, support emails and information you choose to send when asking for help.

Household administrators and other members may supply information about you, even if you do not have a login account. Only enter information you are authorised to share and need for household finance management. Avoid unnecessary sensitive details, bank-login credentials and full payment-card numbers in descriptions or support messages.

Why information is used

Account and financial information is used to provide the service you request: manage household access, store and display records, calculate summaries and projections, and support exports and account management. The operator relies on performance of the service agreement for processing necessary to provide those features to account holders.

The operator relies on legitimate interests in keeping the service secure and reliable for sign-in protection, abuse prevention, security investigations, troubleshooting and operational backups. Records may also concern people who are not account holders; the operator must assess the appropriate basis for that processing rather than treating another member's entry as their consent. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests using the contact above.

Providing account details is necessary to create and secure a login. Financial features depend on the records supplied. We do not use a consent checkbox as the basis for these core activities. The app does not include advertising or analytics trackers. Its financial projections are informational, not automated lending or eligibility decisions.

Who can access information

  • Household members: shared records are visible according to their section permissions. Records marked private are normally visible only to their owner through the ordinary application and export views. Children of a private record inherit its visibility where supported.
  • Shared bank history: marking an income, expense, loan or card private does not hide a linked transaction on a shared bank account. Shared amounts, names and descriptions can still reveal personal details.
  • Household administrators: manage membership and permissions. They do not gain ordinary viewing/export access to another member's private records, but one active administrator can delete the entire household, including everyone's private records, without other members' approval.
  • Operator and site administrators: server/database access and site-level administration can access records across households, including private records. Private visibility is not encryption that hides data from the operator.
  • Service providers: Resend handles outgoing account email; the operator's email service handles support/privacy correspondence. Providers process the information needed for those communications.

Keep downloaded exports secure. The app cannot recall copies downloaded or shared by you or another household member.

Emails and processing outside the UK

Account confirmation, password resets and security alerts are sent through Resend. It processes recipient addresses and message contents, including time-limited account links. Security alerts may contain an IP address, time and browser description. These account messages are not financial exports.

UK application hosting does not mean all processing stays in the UK. Email delivery, support email and provider subprocessors may involve other countries. Applicable processing locations and international-transfer safeguards are not documented in this notice. Contact the operator for current details before relying on UK-only processing.

How long information is kept

  • Accounts and financial records: there is no automatic inactivity-based deletion. Records remain until removed through the app or dealt with following a request. Removing a member from a household is not account or record erasure.
  • Security events: events older than {{ config.SECURITY_EVENT_RETENTION_DAYS }} days are eligible for cleanup. Cleanup runs on sign-in and through scheduled server maintenance; this is not a guarantee of deletion at an exact time.
  • Session records: eligible for cleanup more than 30 days after absolute expiry or revocation. Idle sign-out alone does not immediately remove the record. Individual account deletion removes its session records.
  • Backups and operational logs: no fixed retention period is currently set. Deleted data may remain in backups and operational logs; an immediate purge or a specific final-erasure date cannot be promised.
  • Email and correspondence: provider-held messages and support/privacy correspondence are outside app deletion. Their retention periods are not specified here; contact the operator for details.

Exports and deletion: important differences

Download your data in Account security exports your profile and supported financial fields visible to you. It is not a complete copy of every record about you, a full-household export or a restorable backup. Other members' private records, security logs and some supporting metadata are excluded. Contact the operator if you need a wider personal-data response.

Delete your account removes your login, sessions, recovery codes and privately owned financial domains and their children, including owned records in former households. Shared history remains, with links to deleted records detached. Names and free text are not anonymised. Prior security events remain under normal retention without the user-account link and may still identify you through details such as IP addresses. The last household administrator must appoint another active administrator first; site administrators must have their site role revoked before self-deletion.

Delete this household in Family Management removes all of that household's shared and private records, including records owned by former members. Current members retain their login accounts, passwords and two-factor settings, but membership/permissions are cleared and all their sessions are revoked. Retained security events lose their household link, not their user link. Members can sign back in and use a fresh invite to join another household.

Neither deletion option erases backups, operational logs, email-provider copies or previously downloaded files. Browser display preferences also remain until cleared in the browser.

Your choices and rights

You can edit supported records, change visibility where available, revoke devices, export data and review deletion from the app. You may also contact the operator to request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of personal data, or to object to processing based on legitimate interests. Rights depend on the circumstances and applicable law; in-app export/deletion is not the only way to make a request. We aim to respond within the applicable legal timeframe, normally one month, and explain any lawful extension or refusal.

You can complain to the UK's Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). You can contact us first, but do not have to do so.

Cookies, security and changes

See Cookies and browser storage for sign-in cookies, session limits and display preferences. Password hashing, HTTPS cookie settings, access controls and session revocation help protect information, but cannot eliminate every security risk.

This notice describes the current service and operator choices. The update date will change when the notice is revised. Outstanding provider-transfer and retention arrangements described above still need operator review; this notice is not a claim that those arrangements have been verified.
