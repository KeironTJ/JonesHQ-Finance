Calendar & privacy

Europe/London

Timed events follow London daylight-saving changes. All-day dates stay on their chosen calendar dates. Repeated or nonexistent clock-change times must be replaced with an unambiguous time.

Per-event permissions

Each event has independent edit and delete permissions. The creator and household owners manage shared events; owners never override private visibility. Adult-private events are creator-only; managed-child private events are visible to the child and designated guardian within the same household.

When a creator leaves, private events are removed and shared events remain for household owners to manage.

Shared meal planning

All members with Household Hub access can view meals and plans. Adults manage the shared meal library and planner; managed children view only. Breakfast, lunch and dinner can each contain multiple meals per day.

Use week or month view and add meals straight onto a date. Enter a title, description and recipe source, or optionally copy from the library. Each planned meal is independent: library edits never change existing plans.

Plans appear automatically in the calendar and link back to the planner. Archiving a meal keeps existing plans; restoring it allows new planning. Shared meals and plans remain when a member leaves or deletes their account; their added-by attribution is cleared.

Activities & suggestions

Adults publish and manage shared household activities. Children can suggest activities, revise rejected/pending suggestions or withdraw their own suggestions. Only the creator and household adults can see suggestions until an adult explicitly approves them.

Approved activities appear automatically in the calendar. Attendees are household members and gain no additional permissions. Weekly repeats keep London clock times through the selected last-start date. Editing or deleting changes the entire series, including past dates; individual occurrence exceptions are not available yet.

Activities survive member/account deletion; attendance and added-by/reviewer links are removed.

Tasks & personal lists

Adults manage shared household lists and tasks. Children can complete/reopen only shared tasks assigned to them. All members can manage their own personal lists; a child's designated guardian can also view their personal lists within this household, but cannot change them. Adult personal lists are creator-only, even for household owners.

List privacy is fixed at creation and tasks cannot move lists. Personal lists/tasks are deleted on member/account departure; shared work remains with assignments and attribution detached. Incomplete dated tasks appear in each authorised viewer's calendar; completion removes them and reopening restores them. Today/overdue filters use the London date.

Rewards

Any adult with Household Hub access can set up shared-task reward jobs and approve points or reward requests. Children see their own jobs, progress and goals, not other children's point histories. Personal lists never earn points. Each job earns points once, even if reopened. Reward requests hold points until approved or returned on decline.

Earlier jobs are separate from current work. Adults can reschedule tasks or close jobs without awarding points; finished jobs stay in history. Behaviour ratings, streaks, achievements, recurring tasks and external sharing are not available yet.
