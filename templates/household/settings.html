Calendar & privacy

Europe/London

Timed events follow London daylight-saving changes. All-day dates stay on their chosen calendar dates. Repeated or nonexistent clock-change times must be replaced with an unambiguous time.

Per-event permissions

Each event has independent edit and delete permissions. The creator and household owners manage shared events; owners never override private visibility. Adult-private events are creator-only; managed-child private events are visible to the child and designated guardian within the same household.

When a creator leaves, private events are removed and shared events remain for household owners to manage. External sharing, meals, activities and task feeds are not available yet.
