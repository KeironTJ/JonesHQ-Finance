Expense Reimbursement Groups

Decide how each stream of expenses gets paid back to you.

Back to Expenses
Every expense belongs to exactly one group. A group is either:
  • Separate transaction — the classic flow. All the group's expenses for a pay period are totalled into their own "Expense Reimbursement" transaction.
  • Folded into payslip — instead of a separate transaction, the period's total is added on top of the take-home pay of whichever job/income you choose below. Useful when your employer reimburses expenses with your salary rather than as a separate payment. Only works once a payslip (Income record) exists for that period — run "Generate income" first if it hasn't been created yet. Credit-card expenses in a folded group still need their card payment settled manually, since there's no separate reimbursement transaction to fund it automatically.
Every family starts with one "Default" group (separate mode) that can't be deleted — add more groups if you want to split expenses per job or per claim type.
